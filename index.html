Welcome to Executive Inn & Suites

Executive Inn & Suites is conveniently located off Interstate 495 on Crain Highway in Upper Marlboro, MD. Situated less than 2 miles from Showplace Arena and short drives from FedEx Field, Six Flags America, and Joint Base Andrews, our hotel provides the ideal home base for business executives, event attendees, and vacationing families.

Enjoy clean, quiet rooms equipped with 38-inch LED TVs, coffee makers, hair dryers, irons, microwaves, refrigerators, and endless hot water. Unwind in our luxurious King Jacuzzi Suites or enjoy our weekend continental breakfast!
